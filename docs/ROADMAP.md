# Roadmap

KineScope 0.3 is a broad research substrate. It is not a claim that every manufactured twisty puzzle, polyhedral cut complex, and physically annoying plastic object has been reduced to a solved software abstraction.

## Implemented through Phase 1

- exact rational plane normalization and predicates for the affine-convex specialization;
- deterministic exact convex B-reps and output-sensitive incremental planar clipping;
- canonical atomic sign chambers, exact adjacency, bond quotient, and physical pieces;
- exact `outer-hull`, `cut-surface`, and `internal-surface` provenance;
- exposed surfaces, raw boundary traces, deterministic triangulations, certificates, and SHA-256 hashes;
- independent artifact verifier and `polytwist.affine-geometry.v1` schema;
- direct canonical compilation of Classic, Ghost, Mirror, Axis, bandaged, and procedural planar-cut presets;
- renderer projection from canonical faces, compile-once meshes, and rigid-transform-only state updates;
- exact Rational Ghost Atlas A regression with 27 cells and six proved selected-face traces;

- exact cubic `2×2×2` through `9×9×9` logical mechanisms;
- convex shape-mod compilation from bounded half-spaces;
- rotated and displaced cutting frames;
- custom outer/interior layer generators and action alphabets;
- connected rigid bandages with exact state-dependent legality;
- transactional state loading, preview/commit, undo, redo, and replay;
- deterministic legal scrambles and public state fingerprints;
- interactive WebGL2 and headless deterministic CPU rendering;
- studio, albedo, piece, face, normal, and depth observations;
- dense geometry/topology reports and exact dynamics analysis;
- bounded state graphs and legality-pattern statistics;
- 24 mechanics-frame hypotheses and information-gain experiment ranking;
- 16 benchmark task families and eight named splits;
- public/private episodes, JSON/JSONL suites, targets, verification certificates, and evaluator;
- latency, token, cost, coverage, and calibration reporting;
- stateless REST API, OpenAPI output, batch operations, and Vercel adapters.

## Phase 1 hardening

- add the second rational Ghost Atlas B as a full trace regression;
- strengthen the verifier with independently recomputed volumes and adversarial certificate fixtures;
- add content-addressed artifact persistence instead of process-local caching;
- benchmark coefficient growth and add safe resource ceilings;
- prove and document the cubic compatibility map beyond regression evidence;
- complete renderer manifests from every raster primitive back to exact source faces.

## Phase 2+: richer experimentation and materialization

- streaming dataset generation and artifact-store adapters;
- asynchronous large-suite job API;
- first-class model-run manifests, trace/event JSONL, retries, and failure taxonomy;
- automatic multi-camera and short-video materialization;
- metric depth, optical flow, visibility graphs, and occlusion labels;
- exact shortest-path baselines for bounded components;
- Bayesian posterior updates over broader mechanics hypothesis classes;
- configurable disclosure of legality, identity, notation, and sensor calibration;
- intervention registry for impossible-state and corrupted-transition controls.

## Deferred mechanism work

- swept-volume collision checks only after exact path semantics and certificates are specified;
- lock/unlock and key-piece predicates;
- partial-angle and jumbling turns;
- multi-stage actions and conditional generators;
- true mesh unions and convex decompositions for rigid assemblies;
- mechanically meaningful friction, clearance, and tolerance parameters.

## v0.6: general cut complexes

- non-cubic logical coordinate systems;
- arbitrary plane-rotation generators;
- tetrahedral, octahedral, dodecahedral, corner-turning, and edge-turning families;
- symbolic permutation/orientation compilation independent of cubic coordinates;
- non-convex pieces represented as validated convex decompositions;
- procedural mechanism grammars rather than family-specific constructors.

## v0.7: embodied and sim-to-real

- calibrated camera rigs and real video sequences;
- physically based materials, environments, shadows, blur, and sensor noise;
- scan-to-simulator alignment for physical puzzles;
- robot gripper, contact, and manipulation interfaces;
- partial-observation belief states and uncertainty-aware state estimation;
- domain adaptation and sim-to-real evaluation.

## Near-term paper milestones

1. Freeze one primary active-identification protocol and one secondary transfer protocol.
2. Generate pilot suites and quantify task difficulty before spending model budget enthusiastically and irreversibly.
3. Establish random, fixed-sweep, passive, oracle-information-gain, and exact-state baselines.
4. Validate appearance/mechanics controls and hidden-test leakage boundaries.
5. Run modern multimodal models on IID, geometry-OOD, mechanics-OOD, and compositional-OOD conditions.
6. Perform task and information-regime ablations.
7. Release engine, development splits, schemas, raw predictions, evaluation harness, and reproducibility metadata together.
